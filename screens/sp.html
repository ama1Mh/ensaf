Before you start the camera

Tracking uses the camera to follow your head or eye movement. No video is stored.

  1. 1Click "Allow" in the browser permission window
  2. 2Good lighting — your face should be clearly visible
  3. 3Sit 50–70 cm from the screen
  4. 4Move your head or eyes to steer the cursor
